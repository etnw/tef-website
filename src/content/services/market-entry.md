---
title: "Cross-border market entry"
anchor: "market-entry"
navLabel: "Cross-border market entry"
order: 4
lead: >-
  Commercial representation in a region where the client has no local presence, carried out by a partner based in that region.
blocks:
  - label: "Delivered"
    body: >-
      local market assessment covering buyers, competitors, and purchasing practice. Distribution and channel partner identification, qualification, and negotiation. Customer meetings conducted locally, in the local language. Introductions to regional ecosystem organisations including clusters, investment promotion agencies, and industry bodies. Handover to the client's own local presence when volume justifies one.
  - label: "Not included"
    body: >-
      entity formation, payroll, tax structuring, and immigration.
  - label: "Applies when"
    body: >-
      an opportunity in another region is established but local hiring is premature, or a customer requires local support that does not currently exist.
---
