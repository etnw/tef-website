      customer introductions from an established industry network. Customer meetings and technical presentations, with or without the client present. Pricing discussions and commercial negotiation through to contract signature. Representation inside the customer's own decision process, including steering committees, executive reviews, and supplier qualification boards. Sales enablement material produced during the engagement and handed over at the end: pitch decks, one-pagers, battle cards, competitive comparison matrices, application note structures.
  - label: "Basis"
    body: >-
      the engagement is accountable for the signed contract, not for the recommendation that preceded it. Pricing and positioning decisions are made by the same people who conduct the customer negotiation.
