---
title: "Commercial due diligence"
anchor: "due-diligence"
navLabel: "Commercial due diligence"
order: 8
lead: >-
  Independent assessment of the commercial position of an acquisition target, an investment candidate, or a prospective partner company.
blocks:
  - label: "Delivered"
    body: >-
      market and competitive assessment of the target's served segments. Review of the customer base, pipeline quality, and design-in status, distinguishing committed revenue from opportunity. Pricing and margin analysis. Assessment of channel and distribution arrangements, including contractual exposure. Evaluation of the commercial organisation and what would be required after a transaction. Written findings for an investment committee or board.
  - label: "Applies when"
    body: >-
      evaluating an acquisition or a minority investment, or preparing for commercial integration once a transaction has been agreed.
---
