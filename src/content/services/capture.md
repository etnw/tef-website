RFI, RFQ, and proposal support: response structure, technical and commercial narrative, and compliance against the customer's requirements. Review cycles ahead of submission, and negotiation support afterwards.
  - label: "Applies when"
    body: >-
      the opportunity is awarded through a formal competitive process, the customer is a tier-one, prime contractor, or public buyer with a defined procurement route, or the company has the technology to win and no process for bidding.
