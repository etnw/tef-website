blocks:
  - label: "Delivered"

      market selection and entry sequence, assessed against available runway rather than market size alone. Channel model comparison covering direct, distribution, and representation, with the economics of each. Business case for product investment, including revenue forecast. Assessment of qualification and regulatory requirements where they gate market access. Go-to-market playbook documented for handover to the client's team.
  - label: "Applies when"
    body: >-
      the product roadmap is set and the commercial route is not, or when demand exists across more segments than the company has capacity to serve.
