anchor: "product-strategy"
navLabel: "Product strategy & lifecycle"
order: 2
lead: >-
  Market and competitive intelligence turned into a roadmap, and ownership of the product or product line through its life, not only through its launch.
blocks:
  - label: "Delivered"
    body: >-
      multi-year product roadmap derived from the market analysis rather than from engineering capacity. Prioritisation and investment decisions across a portfolio, with the trade-offs written down. Make or buy assessment covering internal development, licensing, and acquisition. Product requirements defined against customer and market evidence. Pricing and margin position held over the product's life. End-of-life and transition planning. Commercial KPIs, including revenue growth, market share, and pipeline quality, reported on a fixed cadence.
  - label: "Applies when"
    body: >-
      the roadmap is set by what engineering can build rather than by what the market will buy, a portfolio decision has to be made and no one owns it, or a product has a launch team but no owner for the years after it.
---
