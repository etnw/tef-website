---
title: "Cross-functional coordination"
anchor: "coordination"
navLabel: "Cross-functional coordination"
order: 5
lead: >-
  The connective function between Product Management, R&D, Sales, Business Development, and Controlling, where each needs the others to move and none of them reports to the others.
blocks:
  - label: "Delivered"
    body: >-
      a single point of contact accountable for the commercial decision across functions. Requirements reconciled between what customers ask for, what R&D can deliver, and what the business case supports. Launch and design-in activity sequenced across the functions that have to execute it. Escalation of the decisions that are stalling, with a recommendation attached. Reporting that gives management one view of the commercial position rather than one per department.
  - label: "Basis"
    body: >-
      the work is done without formal authority over any of the functions involved. It depends on being credible in each of their languages, which is why it is done by a partner who has held roles in more than one of them.
  - label: "Applies when"
    body: >-
      decisions are made in each function but not between them, a launch slips because no one owns the handoffs, or the answer to a customer question depends on three departments and takes weeks to assemble.
