Rich has 30+ years in semiconductors and power and energy systems, spanning high-volume consumer markets, startups, and full P&L ownership, with a track record of building design-win pipelines and leading integrated product, applications engineering, and engineering teams.

At Cypress Semiconductor, he ran the $100M high-speed USB business unit at 25% profit before tax and drove WirelessUSB, the company's first wireless product line, from conception to production. At Alta Devices, he took the company from stealth to production as Chief Marketing, Product, and Sales Officer, building the commercial organization from the ground up, scaling the go-to-market pipeline more than 10x in the first year, and playing a key leadership role in its successful acquisition. At SiTime, he owned product strategy and growth for the Industrial and Medical business, generating a $30M+ design-in pipeline across 1,000+ target accounts and 40+ applications.

He holds a B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Illinois, Urbana-Champaign, along with executive workshops at UC Berkeley, Harvard Business School, and Stanford University. Bilingual: English, Polish. He is also a drummer in a local cover band and a lifelong Rush fan.
