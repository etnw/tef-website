lead: >-
  Quantitative assessment of the addressable market, the competitive field, the price a product can command, and the performance it has to reach to command it.
blocks:
  - label: "Delivered"
    body: >-
      TAM and SAM model with documented, challengeable assumptions. Competitive landscape and positioning analysis. Pricing benchmarks based on transacted rather than list prices. Customer segmentation and a ranked target account list. Technical differentiation translated into customer-facing arguments. Product definition input: the capability, performance, and price the served segments actually require, fed back to the product team. Written market analysis suitable for a board or investor audience.
  - label: "Applies when"
    body: >-
      preparing a fundraise, setting a product roadmap, or evaluating entry into a new segment.
