lead: >-
  Independent assessment of whether customers will buy the target's product at the price, in the volume, and on the timeline the investment case assumes.
blocks:
  - label: "Delivered"
    body: >-
      Market need, tested in conversations with target customers. TAM and SAM, set against the company's own figures. Competitive position against incumbents and other startups. Path to revenue: design-in and qualification requirements, and realistic time to production volume. Pricing, channel, and whether the team can close production contracts. A short written report: what the evidence supports, assumptions still to be tested, risks ranked by impact, and questions for the founders.
  - label: "Not included"
    body: >-
      Technical, legal, and financial diligence. These stay with the investor's specialists, and we work alongside them.
  - label: "Applies when"
    body: >-
      Evaluating a venture investment, a minority stake, or an acquisition, or preparing for commercial integration once a transaction has been agreed.
  - label: "Sequence"
    body: >-
      After the transaction, the partner who did the diligence can lead the commercial work: a 90-day plan that closes the gaps the diligence found, embedded commercial leadership, and the first sales hire. Nothing gets lost between the deal and the plan.
